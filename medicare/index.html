Who we are

Every journey is personal.
So is our care.

Founded in 2016, Bigfoot Medicare Pte Ltd provides medical and wheelchair transport for individuals, healthcare institutions and nursing homes across Singapore.

We work with old folks’ homes, nursing homes and families to help residents reach hospital appointments and regular treatments. From coordinating with care teams to supporting the journey back to the home, patient comfort and dignity guide the way we work.

Bigfoot Medicare is part of a Singapore-headquartered group whose approach brings together safety, people development and community responsibility.

People & learning

A culture of continuous development

The Group’s BigFoot Global Academy supports internal learning in logistics, warehouse and facilities management, and workplace safety. Structured onboarding and mentor-led guidance help share practical experience across its workforce.

Listening & improvement

Feedback that shapes better service

Across the Group, customer feedback is tracked, reviewed and resolved through a structured service loop. Findings inform operating procedures, staff training and technology improvements.

Community & inclusion

Responsibility beyond the business

The Group’s community work includes food support, youth activities and social inclusion. Its North West CDC V CARE support helps vulnerable seniors and rental-flat residents through essential supplies and volunteer participation.

How do I book a journey?

Use Book an appointment to open our appointment form, or WhatsApp +65 9797 7323 to discuss your needs with our operator. Availability and arrangements are confirmed by the team.

What areas do you cover?

We serve locations across Singapore, providing patient transport between homes and healthcare institutions.

What are your operating hours?

Frontline services operate Monday to Sunday, 7 am to 7 pm. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm.

Can a wheelchair user travel with a companion?

Our wheelchair transport vehicles accommodate wheelchair users and seated passengers. Capacity depends on the vehicle and individual requirements; please confirm the arrangements when booking.

Is this an emergency ambulance service?

Our patient transport services focus on non-emergency journeys. For booking questions or patient support arrangements, speak directly with the team.
