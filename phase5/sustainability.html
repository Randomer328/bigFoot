02 / A greener fleet

Progress, out on the road.

Our fleet investment connects fuel efficiency, vehicle uptime and the sustainability goals of the customers we serve.

4Scania Super trucks
5 yearsRepair & maintenance contracts
Fuel-efficient port operations+

Four Scania Super P420 A4x2NZ trucks support port operations. The Scania Super specification cited in the 2026 submission offers fuel savings of up to 8% versus standard trucks.

Electric vehicle addition+

The addition of an electric vehicle marks a step towards lower-emission logistics and the evaluation of cleaner fleet options.

Maintenance that supports uptime+

Five-year Scania repair and maintenance contracts provide broader parts coverage and access to high-quality components, supporting reliable performance and proactive maintenance.

Fleet intelligence & driver safety+

Vehicle positioning, performance monitoring, service planning, defect reporting and driver insights support efficient operations. ABS and traction control reinforce safety in heavy-duty haulage.

03 / Our core strength

Protect the product.
Respect the resources.

Sustainable cold chain means looking after both the cargo and the resources used to move it. Operational efficiency and product integrity belong in the same conversation.

01 / Plan

Smarter routes.

Route optimisation improves delivery density, reduces fuel consumption and helps avoid unnecessary journeys.

02 / Protect

Less product loss.

Real-time temperature monitoring, inventory visibility and exception management help reduce spoilage and wastage.

03 / Deliver

Less paper. Less waste.

Digital documentation and paperless proof of delivery simplify records. Sustainable packaging and waste-reduction practices support fulfilment and distribution.

Fleet utilisation & reduced idle time+

AI-enabled planning is part of the company’s improvement programme, supporting better vehicle utilisation, fewer idle periods and more carbon-conscious cold chain decisions.

Responsible resource use+

Coordinating storage, handling and refrigerated transport helps protect temperature integrity while improving the use of vehicles, warehouse resources and packaging.
