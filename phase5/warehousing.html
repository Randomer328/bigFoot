Our Expertise

Warehousing

Select a topic to explore the service.

01

Location & Layout

+

Warehouses near industrial areas and air or sea ports support transfers between storage, production and transport. Layouts accommodate the movement of trucks and containers.

02

Storage & Tracking

+

Allocated cargo locations, labelling, barcodes and computerised warehouse management help teams organise goods and trace inventory, from spare parts to larger cargo.

03

Cold Storage

+

Temperature-controlled storage supports perishable products, including food and produce. Space allocation and temperature management are planned around the requirements of the goods.

04

Dry Cargo Management

+

Racks, containers, BIN cards, labelling and inventory systems support dry cargo storage. Inventory visibility and movement records help coordinate order processing, dispatch and the placement of frequently moving stock.
