Freight Forwarding

Select a topic to explore the service.

01

Air Import

+

Air import services combine consolidation and deconsolidation with documentation, customs declarations, local forwarding and door-to-door delivery. Sea-air and air-sea transshipment, break-bulk and specialist cargo requirements can be discussed with the team.

02

Air Export

+

International agents and associates support exports from Singapore and Malaysia. Services include domestic collection, multimodal transshipment, consolidation, documentation, customs declarations and door-to-door arrangements.

03

Sea Import

+

Sea import coordination connects origin collection, transport to port, ocean movement and final delivery. Support includes LCL and FCL consolidation, import documentation, customs declarations and break-bulk handling.

04

Sea Export

+

Sea export services include LCL and FCL movements, consolidation at major ports, port-to-port and door-to-door delivery, customs administration and cargo-space arrangements with ocean carriers.
